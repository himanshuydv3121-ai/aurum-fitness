About AURUM

A club built for people who train on purpose.

A small, private house for strength, conditioning and recovery, run by coaches who stay.

Our story

Started with a single rack.

AURUM opened in 2019 on one floor with six platforms and a simple rule: coach every set. Members came for the bars and stayed for the standard.

Today the club covers 4,200 square metres, but the rule has not changed. Coaching ratios are capped, equipment is calibrated every quarter and the floor is never allowed to feel crowded.

Principles

Three things we do not compromise on.

Precision

Plates are weighed to within 10 g, sessions are logged and every program has a measurable target.

Privacy

Capped membership, private locker suites and no photography on the floor. You train without an audience.

Progression

Every member gets a written block plan and a review every six weeks with their coach.
